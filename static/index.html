Keyword syntax: separate terms with ; for AND across fields, use OR / parentheses, and CPC codes like (A47J). Each search uses 1 SerpApi credit.
USPTO Open Data Portal: US applications filed since 2001, free. It searches bibliographic data only (no abstract or claims). Fields: TI= title, CPC= classification, APP= applicant, INV= inventor (or full names like applicationMetaData.inventionTitle:). Use AND / OR / NOT, * and "quoted phrases", e.g. TI=(capsul* OR pod*) AND TI=(compost* OR biodegrad*). Without a field prefix, words match any field, including addresses.
